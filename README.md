# Distribution after AI

Two short working papers and a public scorecard.

1. Separating Capture from Allocation: A Commitment Model of Distribution after AI (v1.0). When can a promised share of what machines produce actually be kept? English PDF, Chinese translation, source and a verification script.
2. Binding at the Bottleneck: An Enforcement Model of Distribution after AI (v1.0). Where does a distribution rule have to be collected so that it cannot be walked around? English PDF, Chinese translation, source and a verification script.
3. Separation Scorecard v1.1. Four questions any scheme can be assessed against, by itself or by anyone else. English and Chinese. CC BY 4.0.

Criticism is welcome. Open an issue, or submit an assessment of a real scheme under scorecard/assessments/.
